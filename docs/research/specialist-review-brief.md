# Specialist review brief: West Bengal pilot

Prepared 2026-10-12. Draft review packet; not sent and not endorsed. See the [protocol](west-bengal-research-protocol.md), [source catalogue](west-bengal-evidence-catalogue.md), [data contract](research-data-contract.md) and [experimental MRV formula](../08-mrv-module-design.md).

## Requested review

1. Which documented West Bengal context/practice bundle has sufficient independent data for a first study? Should seasonal rice-gas mitigation or multi-year SOC change be the first measurable target, given partner capacity?
2. Are candidate treatments, controls, prior adoption and the monitoring boundary appropriate? Which additionality and imported-amendment/leakage issues need separate assessment?
3. For SOC: what depth/mass basis, core replication, bulk-density/coarse-fragment handling, assay and laboratory QA are required? What change and follow-up duration are detectable with the proposed budget?
4. For rice gases: what sampling/integration protocol captures irrigation/fertiliser/rainfall events, and how must CH4/N2O and yield tradeoffs be assessed?
5. Do local source tables report carbon stock, stock change, retention efficiency or emissions? Are units/time bases and treatment bundles transferable to the selected context?
6. Is there a defensible reason for the current NDVI multiplier? Which independent adoption/stock labels can test it, and what weather/crop/sensor confounding should be modelled?
7. Which model/comparator, site/block holdout and uncertainty methods are appropriate? Define acceptable errors and subgroup support before holdout evaluation.
