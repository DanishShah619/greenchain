For each usable study, complete a source record: authors/year/DOI; full-text location and access date; table/figure identity; site/soil/crop/rotation/season; treatment bundle and control; management history; start/end dates; SOC depth and bulk-density method or gas integration method; raw and transformed units; replicates; uncertainty type/covariance; licence and permitted reuse; data availability; applicability/exclusions; reviewer and review date.

Classification labels: VERIFIED_BIBLIOGRAPHY, FULL_TEXT_AUDITED, RAW_DATA_USABLE, REPRODUCED, INDEPENDENT_HOLDOUT_EVALUATED. A record can reach the first status without reaching the others. No West Bengal record currently reaches INDEPENDENT_HOLDOUT_EVALUATED.
